# Play Your Color

Play Your Color is a personalized game-discovery experience that helps players understand what they enjoy and find games that fit the way they play.

Rather than asking players to start with genres alone, Play Your Color explores the motivations, worlds, mechanics, moods, and experiences that make a game memorable. The outcome is a personal Gaming Color, a detailed Gaming DNA profile, and recommendations designed around the player—not a generic popularity list.

## The Product

Every player approaches games differently. Some are drawn to mastery and challenge; others want exploration, creativity, stories, connection, or freedom. Play Your Color turns those preferences into a clear, approachable profile that makes discovering the next great game feel more personal.

The experience is built around six Gaming Colors, each representing a different play style. A player receives a primary color, a secondary influence, and a fuller view of how their preferences work together.

## The Player Journey

### 1. Discover Your Color

Players answer a guided set of questions about how they like to play, what motivates them, and the kinds of moments they enjoy most.

### 2. Explore Your Gaming DNA

The results reveal more than a single label. Players can see the motivations that shape their play style, their visual and world preferences, preferred gameplay activities, presentation choices, and ideal game experience.

### 3. Find Your Next Game

Personalized recommendations highlight games that align with the player’s profile. Each recommendation includes a match score and a clear explanation of why it may be a good fit.

### 4. Browse With Intention

The Explore Games experience lets players browse the catalogue by the qualities that matter to them—such as visual style, camera, world structure, gameplay, and social play—while keeping their personal match in view.

### 5. Understand Every Match

Each game has its own detail page, where players can review its profile, see how it compares with their Gaming DNA, and understand where it is likely to feel especially aligned or a little outside their usual comfort zone.

## What Players Receive

- A primary and secondary Gaming Color
- A visual breakdown of core gaming motivations
- A personalized Gaming DNA profile
- Preference insights across visuals, presentation, worlds, gameplay, and experience
- Curated game recommendations with match explanations
- A selection of unexpected games worth exploring
- A filterable game catalogue for continued discovery

## Who It’s For

Play Your Color is for players who want better ways to describe their taste, discover games beyond the genres they already know, or find a thoughtful starting point when choosing what to play next.

It is equally useful for someone new to games, someone with a long backlog, or someone who simply wants an answer to: *“What should I play next?”*

## Product Principles

Play Your Color is designed to be:

- Personal — recommendations are grounded in the player’s individual preferences.
- Clear — results explain the reasoning behind each match.
- Curious — discovery includes both familiar fits and surprising possibilities.
- Respectful — there are no right or wrong answers; every play style is valid.
- Useful — profiles are meant to help players make confident choices, not just provide a label.

## Experience Areas

| Area | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Introduces the Play Your Color experience and starts the assessment. |
| Assessment | Helps players express their gaming motivations and preferences. |
| Results | Presents the player’s color, Gaming DNA, and personalized matches. |
| Explore Games | Supports browsing and filtering across the game catalogue. |
| Game Detail | Explains a specific game and its relationship to the player’s profile. |

## A Note on Recommendations

Recommendations are intended as a discovery guide, not a final verdict. A strong match suggests that a game aligns with the player’s stated preferences; an unexpected match can offer a rewarding new perspective. The best next game is ultimately the one that feels right to the player.
